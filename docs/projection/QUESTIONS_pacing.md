# Questions and defaults (pacing)

1. **Which revenue basis is the goal on?** The plan's revenue is net of an estimated sales tax but not of refunds; refunds run about 11% of DTC revenue in early November. Default chosen: keep the sheet's basis for the pace and show the refunds as a memo row. On a net-of-refunds basis November revenue paces about 101%, not 106%. Needs Josh's call, then one of: deduct a refund-rate lever in revenue pacing, or restate the goal.
2. **Two copies of the Payback inputs.** `drivers.payback` (product_cost_pct, fulfillment_per_order, horizon_months) and `financing.payback` (product_cost_pct, fulfillment, max_age) both hold the sheet's Payback!D11 / B12 / 36. Default chosen: pacing reads `financing.payback` first, then `drivers.payback`, then the sheet value, and the Variables panel writes to `financing.payback`. One of the two should be removed by its owner.
3. **Plan-block definitions (for proj-core / pnl).** Pacing reads P&L goals by sheet row through `pnl.lines.ROW_TO_KEY` (rows 11-13, 17-22, 24-47, 51-66, 75-91, 94, 100 as monthly series; rows 14-16, cost per customer, order value and return on ad spend, come from the drivers result). All were present when this was written. If a key is renamed, `TargetBook.missing_rows()` lists the gap and the page shows "Plan lines missing".
4. **Hourly Meta spend curve.** Typed in the sheet (Automated Pacing L23:M46) and loaded by `seed-hourly`; it has no editor. Default chosen: leave as a loaded input; say if it should be a variable with an editor, or derived from a Meta time-of-day pull.
5. **Retention block of Month Snapshot (J5:Q19).** Not built; it needs `CohortResult.cohort_rows` with the per-cohort cumulative table. Default chosen: omit; the Cohorts page shows the live curve against the goal curve.
6. **Core guard for edits.** `PUT /levers` and `PUT /rules` use the core guards (JSON only, Origin, rate limit) and write `proj_audit`; rules have no scenario and no history snapshot (restore does not cover them).
