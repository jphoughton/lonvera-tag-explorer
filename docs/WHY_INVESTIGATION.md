# The `why` investigation

`why` (brain MCP tool, `brain/why.py`) walks the metric tree, then runs an investigation (`brain/why_investigate.py`, statistics in `brain/why_stats_inv.py`). Deterministic SQL and arithmetic, no model call, so a question stays cheap. Own budget: 36 extra queries and 60 seconds on top of `max_steps` (was 24 / 45; the six hypotheses added in `brain/why_more.py` use about 12 more queries, repeated ones are cached).

## What it returns (read in this order)
- `answer`: the cause with its confidence word, or "inside noise at every grain tested". `confidence`: supported by control / consistent with / inside noise / unexplained.
- `chain`: why -> why -> why with numbers: L0 the move and its grain, L1 spend vs new-customer revenue, L2 hypotheses still standing, L3 product and ad sets carrying it, L4 the change someone made with its control.
- `ruled_out`, `untestable` (names the missing data), `next_check` (the one test or the next day's value that settles it), `action` only when a control supports it, `depth_stopped_at`.
- `noise_gate`: the move at 1, 3 and 7 days against the same weekdays of the prior 4 weeks (noise band = 2 x the larger of the Poisson band on new customers (or clicks for CPM / CTR / CPC) and the empirical spread of those baseline windows), what one more day would need to show, and the hour-of-day curve (orders_hourly, cumulative new-customer revenue vs the same weekday).
- `ledger`: one verdict for every hypothesis: H1 attribution lag, H2 spend level, H3 mix by product and stage, H4 CPM / CTR / CPC, H5 new vs old ad families, H6 funnel steps by product (binomial z), H7 AOV decomposed exactly into pack size, product mix, price point and discount (order lines, new customers, product net, shipping and tax excluded), H8 returning-buyer contamination, H9 country mix, H10 breakdown segments, H11 email / SMS, H12 stockouts, H13 feed breaks, plus six added later: H1b attribution leak (sub-test of H1), H4b cap and floor exposure, H14 persona / tag mix, H16 intraday shape, H17 order mix, H18 calendar (each described below); then E1.. one per material Meta change event with its control, and C.. coincident page / Klaviyo / price / ad events (before / after only).
- Verdict words: `supported by control` (edited vs unedited objects differ beyond noise, parallel pre-trend, at least 5% of spend; still not randomized), `consistent with`, `ruled out`, `inside noise`, `untestable`.
- `priors_seen_before`: earlier runs from `analytics.why_findings` (a lead, never proof).

## The six added hypotheses (each one verdict; evidence shows material movers only, full tables in `detail`)
- **H14 persona / tag mix.** `creative_family_daily` (basis `upstack_new_customers`, all ads) joined to the current tracker tags (persona / ICP, pain point, style, type), segments keyed by product + tag value. Exact shift-share of the move in NC ROAS, cost per new customer or conversion (whichever the question is) into a MIX part (the segment's share of spend / new customers / clicks moved, valued at its own comparison rate against the total) and a RATE part (its own rate moved, at its current share); mix + rate = the total move with no residual (unit tested). A mover has a mix or rate part of at least 10% of the move and either a share shift of 3+ points or a rate change beyond 2 x sqrt(1/NC cur + 1/NC cmp) with 10+ new customers each side. The top mover's ad sets are matched to the change timeline. Caveats printed every time: tags are today's tags applied to past days; if the window ends within 7 days of the last loaded day Upstack attribution is still maturing, so only share shifts are read. Hook type is untestable (the tracker stores hook text, no type). This basis is the ad views' Upstack any-click credit, not orders_daily, and says so.
- **H4b cap and floor exposure.** `meta_bids_daily` (from 2026-04-28, account-level): share of spend under a cost / bid cap and under a ROAS floor, spend-weighted cap and floor level, Meta-reported cost per purchase against the cap and Meta-reported return against the floor (binding = within 15%). `meta_adset_state_daily` is only used to say whether snapshots cover the windows (it starts 2026-11-12); before that the activity-log events (E1..) carry the edits.
- **H1b attribution leak.** New customers tied to no Meta ad (`attribution_unmapped_daily`, share of actual new customers) and the non-Meta share of last-click new customers (`attribution_source_daily`), binomial z, flagged at |z| >= 3 and 1+ point. Unmapped orders sit inside orders_daily, so they never move the actual-orders headline, only ad-level ROAS.
- **H16 intraday.** Cumulative share of the target day's new-customer revenue (`orders_hourly`) and spend (latest `pacing_live_today` snapshot per hour) by hour against the same weekday of the prior 4 weeks, band = 2 x the spread of those shares (floor 2 points). Reports front- or back-loaded, the first hour outside the band and logged changes within an hour of it. Tested on the window's last day; a day still running is read on cumulative revenue only and can still be restated.
- **H17 order mix.** New-customer orders: share by `source_name`, cancellation rate, rebill-flagged share, share with a refund (binomial z) and refunded amount / total_price. Pack size and price point are the H7 decomposition, not repeated. `source_name` values are Shopify app ids: `web` is the online store, `tiktok` and `shopify_draft_order` read as named, the numeric ids are not named anywhere in the data, so what they are is untestable-meaning. No discount-code test: codes sit on 0-1.6% of new-customer orders.
- **H18 calendar.** Static calendar (US federal holidays, Black Friday, Cyber Monday, BFCM ramp Nov 15 to Dec 2, month start days 1-3, paydays 1st / 15th / last business day). Flags days in one window and not the other, then asks whether the same calendar position one month and one year earlier (both windows moved back, 364 days for the year) moved the metric the same way, on orders_daily and platform spend since 2025-01-01. Wording is "consistent with a calendar effect" at most.

## The edited-vs-unedited control
For a Meta ad set / campaign event: the affected ids vs every other ad set on `meta_ad_daily`, 3 days before vs from the event day to the end of the window: spend, CPM, CPC, conversion (Upstack new customers per link click), NC ROAS, CPA per group, pre-trend of each group, difference in differences. "Consistent with not the cause" is the wording when the unedited group moved the same way. Time-stamped events also get a before / after split of the event day from `orders_hourly` (account level only; ad-set hourly is not stored).

## Tables and views
`analytics.change_timeline` (migration 261): Meta activity-log edits with bulk edits grouped (same event type, same actor, within 90 minutes -> one row, ids in `object_ids`), landing page copy / price changes, Klaviyo flows and campaigns, new ad families, first-seen discount codes and price points, stockouts, failed ETL runs. `public.why_findings` / `analytics.why_findings`: written by the hosted brain after each run with a fixed parameterized INSERT through `WHY_FINDINGS_URL` (else `DATABASE_URL`); not written (and the answer says so) when neither is set.

## Not available, so untestable
Payment declines and method, split-test visitor assignment, ad-set hourly delivery (Phase 3, not built), Upstack new-customer attribution by placement / device / age / country, email attributed to new customers.
