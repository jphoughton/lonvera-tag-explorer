# Module brief: pacing (P3) — migration 170, `app/projection/pacing/`, page `pacing.js`

Replaces `Automated Pacing` (EBITDA & NOI pacing for the current month vs the P&L goal), `Month Snapshot`, `Pacing - Daily (180d)`, `Rollup - Weekly (WoW)`, `Rollup - Monthly (MoM)`, `Meta - Daily (180d)`, `Meta - Weekly (WoW)`, `Meta - Monthly (MoM)`. Read-only consumer of engine results (P&L targets) and actual feeds; no downstream consumers.

## What the sheet does (read the snapshot formulas; ma-calano.md lists the band history)
- `Automated Pacing`: per metric (revenue, DTC revenue, NC revenue, repeat revenue, NC ROAS, NC CPA, Amazon revenue, sales tax est., COGS lines, advertising by channel Meta/Google/Amazon/TikTok, EBITDA, NOI): Pacing % = projection ÷ goal, MTD actual, last-7-day average, daily needed, MTD-should-be (goal × share of month elapsed), EOM goal (INDEX of `P&L` for the month), EOM projection (MTD ÷ days elapsed × days in month; full days elapsed = `MAX(1, DAY(TODAY())-1)`). Revenue is net of the 7% sales-tax estimate (`Sales tax (est.)` line; DTC rows stay gross). Sources: `Orders - History` (NC / repeat revenue by day), `Amazon - Daily`, `Meta Campaign - History`, `Ad Spend - Daily` (Google), P&L cost-rate columns. A payback box (J4:M10) = projected payback on MTD NC-AOV/CPA with the live curve vs the goal curve.
- `Month Snapshot`: month dropdown → Actual / Projected* / Target / Diff / Diff % per metric (target = `P&L` for the month), payback rows 27–28.
- Rollups: Daily (180d), Weekly (ISO week), Monthly: META block (spend, impressions, reach, first-time impression %, cost per 1,000 reached, CPM, frequency, unique outbound clicks, unique outbound CTR, platform ROAS), GOOGLE, AMAZON; nine per-product metric bands (spend, NC revenue, NC ROAS, NC orders, NC CPA, unique outbound clicks, conversion rate, NC revenue per unique outbound click, cost per unique outbound click) × VitaBull | Beef Liver | Boneflex | Total; TESTING vs SCALING band (Meta spend by stage, testing % of spend, NC by stage from Upstack any-click 30-day by campaign, NC CPA by stage, testing premium); monthly projection block per metric `Projected* | Target | % Diff`. NC revenue/orders/CPA/ROAS are ACTUAL Shopify/Upstack, not Meta-attributed. Known live bug: partial-month Projected* uses DAY(month_end) as multiplier; implement correctly (days in month) and list as a difference.
- Raw feed tabs (hidden): `Ad Spend - Daily`, `Orders - Daily (NC-RC Rev)`, `Attribution - Daily`, `Amazon - Daily`, `*- History`, `Pacing - By Product`, `Meta Campaign - History`, `Meta Spend - By Product`, `Meta Product - Reach`, `NC Rev - By Product`, `Upstack Any-Click - By Campaign`, `Meta Account - Reach`, `Meta Bids - Daily`, `Traffic - Daily` — written by sync jobs. Meta spend/purchases/revenue/impressions/clicks by ad × day already live in this app's `ad_daily`/`family_daily`/`meta_campaigns` (stage = 'test' in campaign name → Testing, else Scaling). Reach is not additive: keep the sheet's day/week/month raw reach rows.

## Build
1. Feeds: for what the app already has (Meta spend, purchases, revenue, clicks, stage by campaign, product from the tag/campaign mapping) query Postgres. For the rest (Upstack new-customer revenue/orders by day and by product, Amazon daily, Google spend, Meta reach, Upstack any-click by campaign, traffic) the ETL `etl/projection_pacing.py` reads the sheet's raw tabs READ-ONLY through the service account (`GOOGLE_SA_JSON` env on the etl service) into `proj_feed_*` tables (daily grain, idempotent upsert on the last 14 days, full backfill option). No Upstack/ExpandFi/Klaviyo credentials in the app.
2. Engine: `pacing(month, as_of)` (MTD, EOM projection, pacing %, daily needed vs P&L goal for the selected scenario), `snapshot(month)`, `rollup(grain, from, to, group)` with the band definitions in a declarative table (band, metric, formula, source) so adding a band is data, not code; per-product bands via product tags; targets from `PnlResult`.
3. API `/api/projection/pacing/…`: `month?month=&scenario=`, `snapshot`, `rollup?grain=day|week|month&bands=`, `freshness` (last date per feed).
4. Page: month pacing table with pacing bars (green ≥ 100%, amber 90–100%, red <90% for revenue/profit; inverse for costs), month dropdown (Month Snapshot), rollup grid with band toggles and the grain switch (note: the existing Tag Explorer already has a Day/Week/Month grain control; reuse its control component if it fits), feed freshness chips, payback box.
5. Reconcile map: `Automated Pacing` col C..H rows 4–23 at the snapshot date, `Month Snapshot` B..F, Monthly rollup columns for closed months (exact to the cent where the feed is the same), Daily/Weekly spot checks. Differences: partial-month projection bug, TODAY()-relative windows.
