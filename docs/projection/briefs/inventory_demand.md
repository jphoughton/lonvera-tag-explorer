# Module brief: inventory_demand (P2) — migration 140, `app/projection/inventory_demand/`, page `inventory_demand.js`

Replaces the demand half of the inventory tabs: `Warehance Daily`, `Warehance Weekly`, `Warehance Fcst Inputs`, `Warehance Demand Fcst`, `Amazon Monthly Units`, `Amazon Daily Units`, `Amazon Fcst Inputs`, `Amazon Demand Fcst`, `Intl Daily Units`, `Intl By Country`, `Intl Fcst Inputs`, `Intl Demand Fcst`, `Total Demand Fcst` (all hidden except through the burn tabs). Output `DemandResult.units[(location, product)]` = units per month for locations `revamp` (US 3PL, Revamp Utah via Warehance), `amazon` (FBA), `intl` (international 3PL); products Vital Bull, Beef Liver, Boneflex (+ Vital Belle, Vital Flow where the sheet carries them). Consumed by inventory_supply.

## What the sheet does (read formulas in the snapshot; see also ma-calano.md "Warehance demand + inventory tabs")
- Daily units shipped by product from Warehance (since 2026-07-17; real volume from 2026-07-27), weekly SUMIFS rollup, `Fcst Inputs` (yellow): baseline window (28 days Warehance, 30 days Amazon/Intl), growth %, 12 seasonality indices, on-hand snapshot (B35:C39 on the Warehance inputs tab), allocated; forecast = baseline rate × growth × seasonality for 36 months from `Warehance Demand Fcst!B3` (rolling start `=DATE(YEAR(TODAY()),MONTH(TODAY()),1)`); current month = MTD actual + rate × remaining days. Demand is flat (growth 0%, seasonality 1.00) by Josh's inputs.
- Amazon: ExpandFi `sales` table (Amazon.com + B2B, Vine excluded, keyed on purchase_date, ~2-day lag). Intl: Josh's monthly `Lonvera-Tracking-YYYY-MM.xlsx` exports (one tab per ship day) for the overseas 3PL; since 2026-09-16 the days are ESTIMATES = Warehance non-US orders × 0.89 (grey italics in the sheet). `Intl By Country` splits UK 36%, AU 22%, CA 12%, rest.
- Shipments from Warehance for non-US orders are auto-cancelled in Warehance (Shopify routes them to the intl 3PL): a Warehance "cancelled" order with a non-US ship-to is not cancelled. Forecast uses shipped units only (correct).

## Build
1. Tables: `proj_units_daily(location, product, day, units, source, is_estimate)`, `proj_demand_inputs(location, product, baseline_days, growth_pct, season jsonb(12), on_hand, allocated, as_of)` (per-location, per-product; seed from the Fcst Inputs tabs), `proj_demand_snapshot` (stock on hand by location/product/date; inventory_supply reads the latest). Loaders in `etl/projection_inventory_demand.py`: (a) seed from snapshot; (b) a READ-ONLY refresher that reads the sheet's `Warehance Daily`, `Amazon Daily Units`, `Intl Daily Units` tabs through the service account (the sync jobs keep writing those tabs; the app does not call Warehance/ExpandFi directly in v1; no keys in code); (c) CSV upload for the monthly intl tracking export.
2. Engine: baseline = trailing N days average per product per location ending at the last complete day (partial last day excluded and flagged), × growth × seasonality; MTD-actual for the current month; per-product weekly view; levers per location/product in scenario params `inventory_demand` (baseline_days, growth_pct, seasonality[12], plus an optional "tie to NC revenue growth" switch OFF by default). Output 60-month `units` series (history months from daily actuals, forward from forecast).
3. API `/api/projection/inventory-demand/…`: `table` (units by product × location by month incl. totals and share of total), `daily?location=&product=&days=` (series for a chart), `inputs` GET/PUT (scenario params), `upload-intl`.
4. Page: demand grid (inputs yellow), daily units chart with baseline overlay, location mix as a stacked bar, data-freshness chip per location (last day loaded, estimated days flagged).
5. Reconcile map: `Warehance Demand Fcst`, `Amazon Demand Fcst`, `Intl Demand Fcst`, `Total Demand Fcst` rows × month columns (match months by the header dates), and the derived baselines (`Fcst Inputs` B5/B6). Differences expected where TODAY() moves the start month; reconcile against the frozen date 2026-11-03.
