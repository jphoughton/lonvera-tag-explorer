# Questions and assumptions (pnl)

Each default below is what the module does today. A change is one line unless noted.

1. **Per-cell editing in the shared grid (needs foundation).** `lib/grid.js` makes every month of an `input:true` row editable, QuickBooks months included, and paints the whole row amber. The P&L has 18 loaded months that must not be edited, so the main grid is read-only (`input:false` on every row) and a projected month is typed over from the line's Change dialog ("Single months typed over", saved as `pnl.<line>` kind `override`, audited). If the grid gained a per-column gate (for example `row.input = 'projected'`, or editing only where `status[i] === 'projected'`) the cells could be edited where they sit, and `table_payload` would flip `input` back on (one line in `views.py`).
2. **Jul-26 is loaded as actual.** The sheet flags Jul-26 "Projected" but holds typed cost values for it (some blank: Customer service, Interest expense). They are loaded into `proj_actuals` as `pnl.*` with source "typed in the Lonvera Projections P&L, Jul-26 (not a QuickBooks close)". That makes the P&L's actual boundary Jul-26, the trailing window Apr to Jul-26 (the sheet's R:U) and the first projected month Aug-26. Replace Jul-26 with the real close from the next QuickBooks upload (preview shows the diff).
3. **Freshness rule.** `etl.projection_pnl.freshness` reports `data_through` = last day of the last loaded month and `stale_after_days` = the freshness setting (45 days, editable under Settings, global) plus the length of the first month that is not loaded, so the shared check (today minus data_through greater than stale_after_days) turns stale when the first unloaded month closed more than 45 days ago. The cohorts module uses a flat 45 days after the last loaded month's end (two weeks of grace for the month after). Say if one rule should win.
4. **Sales tax sign and revenue source.** `sales_tax` is negative (as the sheet) and `total_revenue = dtc + amazon + tiktok + sales_tax`. For months with a loaded QuickBooks DTC value the P&L uses the books (tax 0); for others it calls `drivers.revenue.revenue_lines` (agreed with the drivers agent) with a local fallback of the same rules.
5. **Marketing channel split for Aug and Sep 2026.** The sheet leaves rows 52-55 blank for those two months; the drivers module splits the typed total by its channel shares, so the four channel lines differ from the sheet there (total marketing spend ties).
6. **Forward COGS price assumption.** Cost of goods % of revenue = blended landed cost per bottle (70% US at $3.835, 30% China at $2.82, sales-mix weighted, $3.53) divided by an average price per bottle. The sheet divides by $29.22, labelled "Forward Looking Avg Sales Price Per Bottle" (the old average), not today's $21.62. That gives 12.08%; at $21.62 it is 16.33%. On FY27 revenue of $10.3M that is $0.44M of cost, 29% of FY27 net operating income. Kept as the sheet has it; both prices are settings (Settings card) and the page shows the rate at both.
7. **Marketing software rate window.** The sheet's rate divides Apr-Jul-26 software cost by Feb-May-26 revenue (`=sum(R63:U63)/sum(P22:S22)`). The engine divides by Apr-Jul revenue, so projected months run 2.07% (about $83 to $123 a month) above the sheet. Treated as a sheet bug; the reconcile map allows 2.5% for that line only.
8. **FY2026 net operating income.** The sheet's `BM100` is `=sum(W100:Z100)` (Sep-Dec only) while every other FY2026 cell is the full year. The engine reports the full year ($2.67M), so FY2026 net operating income is not in the reconcile map. Sep-Dec-26 alone is `sum(noi[Sep..Dec])` on the page's monthly row.
9. **Inflation levers.** `P&L!A4:A5` (Opex and COGS inflation, both 0) are referenced by no formula in the sheet. They are implemented as annual rates (`settings.engine.opex_inflation`, `cogs_inflation`) compounding monthly from the first projected month on fixed, carried, averaged and rate-driven lines (product cost inflation applies to China and US COGS only). Default 0, so nothing moves.
10. **Not rebuilt (dead in the sheet).** `P&L!B94` (`#REF!` to a deleted tab), the unlabelled row 34 (always blank), scratch cells `W164:W169`, `A6`/`A7`/`A10` override and haircut labels, `C23`. Fixed Expenses (vendor retainers) is kept as a read-only reference table, as the sheet never wired it in.
