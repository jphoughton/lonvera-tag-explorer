# Lonvera: ads, creative, email, pricing, site

## Standing lesson (kept visible, refresh 2026-11-09)

A causal claim needs a like-for-like control, and counts are not rates. Origin: Oct 21-23 2026, the October repeat miss was called "mostly timing" for two days on a model of how much timing could explain, then the billing-reminder email Josh raised turned out to be the larger driver, then a 50/50 holdout was misread because the no-email side was only visible through a tag that ran from about Oct 15 (L2 items L2-150, L2-157, L2-158, L2-159, L2-161). Rules since then (global rule 13): list every change in the window first, test against a prior-cohort-at-same-age or holdout control, quote rates with denominators (cancels per rebill due, not cancels per week), validate exposure and contamination before reading an experiment, say "consistent with" unless the control is randomized and clean, and run a skeptic pass before Josh acts. The Oct 26 free-fall why-chain applied it: spend -63%% was verified as about 3/4 of the NC fall, and several early claims were withdrawn (PayPal halved, no exclusions, checkout fault).
